📖 WBCS Syllabus (Prelims + Mains)

  • Paper 1: Bengali & English Language
  • Paper 2: General Science
  • Paper 3: Mathematics & Reasoning
  • Paper 4: History, Geography, Indian Polity
  • Paper 5: Current Affairs & GK
