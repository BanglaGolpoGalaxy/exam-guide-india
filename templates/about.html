About Exam Guide India

Our mission is to make quality government exam preparation free, accessible and effective for every aspirant.

Who We Are

Exam Guide India is a free digital exam preparation platform built specifically for aspirants in West Bengal and across India. We focus on competitive government exams including WBCS, PSC Miscellaneous, PSC Clerkship, SSC CGL, SSC CHSL, SSC GD, SSC MTS, Railway NTPC, Railway Group D, Railway Technician, and West Bengal Police (SI and Constable).

We believe that quality exam preparation should not depend on one's financial background. Our entire platform — including notes, mock tests, syllabus guides, and exam notifications — is 100% free and will remain so.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide accurate, up-to-date, and comprehensive exam guidance to every aspirant — regardless of location or economic background.

👁️

Our Vision

To be the most trusted free government exam preparation resource for Bengali-medium and English-medium aspirants in India.
